- **Creator:** [Katrin Bolovtsova](https://www.pexels.com/@ekaterina-bolovtsova/)
- **License:** [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). The official page marks the clip free to use; Pexels allows modification and use on websites/apps. Credit is not required; credit is included on the landing page.
- **Local assets:** `public/landing-judge-gavel.mp4` (muted, 720px wide, 24 fps, optimized) and `public/landing-judge-gavel-poster.webp` (poster frame).
- **Interaction:** muted playback begins when the scene scrolls into view, pauses when it leaves view, and may be replayed with the visible button. The app respects reduced-motion preferences.
